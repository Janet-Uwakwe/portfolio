About

Turning complexity into clarity

I serve as an Executive Operations Specialist to assist founders and businesses that are expanding with the aim of streamlining their operations, simplifying their workflows, and cutting down on repetitive tasks.

I bring together executive support — such as calendar, inbox & travel management — with operations management, systems thinking, and AI-powered automation in order to come up with practical solutions which help businesses to operate more efficiently.

I enjoy taking the idea that "there has to be a better way to do this" and turning it into an actual system — by organizing operations, drawing up standard operating procedures, and designing automated workflows using Notion, Airtable, Make, Zapier, and n8n.

I am curious, pay close attention to detail, and always seek ways to make work simpler, wiser, and more intentional.

Ready to stop stressing and start automating? I'd love to offer you a free systems audit. Together, we'll identify your biggest bottlenecks and map out a solution that actually works.
